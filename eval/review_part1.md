## a04: Handbook wording

**Q:** What does the Consumer Duty obligation to act in good faith require?

**Gold:** PRIN 2A.2.1, PRIN 2A.2.2

**Acceptable answer:** A firm must act in good faith towards retail customers: honesty, fair and open dealing, and acting consistently with retail customers' reasonable expectations.

> **PRIN 2A.2.1R:** Act in good faith A firm must act in good faith towards retail customers.

> **PRIN 2A.2.2R:** Acting in good faith is a standard of conduct characterised by honesty, fair and open dealing and acting consistently with the reasonable expectations of retail customers.

## a07: Handbook wording

**Q:** Who must a firm appoint to have responsibility for oversight of its compliance with DISP 1?

**Gold:** DISP 1.3.7

**Acceptable answer:** An individual at the firm or in its group who carries out an FCA governing function; if there is none, an individual of appropriate seniority.

> **DISP 1.3.7R:** (1) A firm must appoint an individual at the firm, or in the same group as the firm, to have responsibility for oversight of the firm's compliance with DISP 1. (2) The individual appointed must be carrying out a FCA governing function at the firm or in the same group as the firm. (3) If there are no individuals at the firm or in the same group as the firm within (2), the firm must appoint an individual of appropriate seniority. (4) A person approved to perform the limited scope function for the firm or for a firm in the same group as the firm satisfies the condition in (3).

**Q:** Can a firm refuse to look at a complaint because it is too old?

**Gold:** DISP 1.8.1

**Acceptable answer:** Yes, if the complaint is outside the time limits for referral to the Financial Ombudsman Service it may reject it without considering the merits, but it must explain this in a final response.

> **DISP 1.8.1R:** If a respondent receives a complaint which is outside the time limits for referral to the Financial Ombudsman Service (see DISP 2.8) it may reject the complaint without considering the merits, but must explain this to the complainant in a final response in accordance with DISP 1.6.2 R or DISP 1.6.2AR.

## a19: how a customer would ask

**Q:** Does the Consumer Duty mean a firm has to act as my fiduciary?

**Gold:** PRIN 2A.1.11, PRIN 2A.2.4

**Acceptable answer:** No. The Duty does not create a fiduciary relationship where one would not otherwise exist.

> **PRIN 2A.1.11G:** The Consumer Duty does not change the nature of a firm’s relationship with any given retail customer. In particular, it does not create a fiduciary relationship where one would not otherwise exist nor require a firm to provide advice or carry out any other regulated activity where it would not otherwise have done so.

> **PRIN 2A.2.4G:** Acting in good faith does not mean a firm is prevented from pursuing legitimate commercial interests or seeking a profit, provided it does so in a manner which is compliant with the Consumer Duty. Acting in good faith does not require a firm to act in a fiduciary capacity where it was not already obliged to do so.

## a28: how a customer would ask

**Q:** Does a firm have to look for patterns in its complaints and fix the underlying problems?

**Gold:** DISP 1.3.3

**Acceptable answer:** Yes. It must have management controls to identify and remedy recurring or systemic problems, for example by analysing root causes, checking whether they affect other products, and correcting them where reasonable.

> **DISP 1.3.3R:** A respondent must put in place appropriate management controls and take reasonable steps to ensure that in handling complaints it identifies and remedies any recurring or systemic problems, for example, by: (1) analysing the causes of individual complaints so as to identify root causes common to types of complaint; (2) considering whether such root causes may also affect other processes or products, including those not directly complained of; and (3) correcting, where reasonable to do so, such root causes.

## a32: needs every listed provision

**Q:** How often must a manufacturer check that its product is still fair value, and what must it do if it is not?

**Gold:** PRIN 2A.4.24, PRIN 2A.4.25 (all needed)

**Acceptable answer:** Regularly, throughout the product's life. If it no longer provides fair value, it must act to mitigate and where appropriate remediate harm to existing customers and prevent harm to new ones.

> **PRIN 2A.4.24R:** Reviewing the value assessment (1) A manufacturer must regularly review the value assessment throughout the life of the product to ensure that the product continues to provide fair value to retail customers in the target market. (2) A distributor must regularly review its distribution arrangements throughout the life of the product to ensure that they remain consistent with the product providing fair value to retail customers in the target market.

> **PRIN 2A.4.25R:** Where a manufacturer identifies in its review of its value assessment that the product no longer provides fair value, it must take appropriate action to: (1) mitigate, and where appropriate, remediate any harm caused to existing retail customers; and (2) prevent harm to new retail customers.

## a35: needs every listed provision

**Q:** Under what conditions can a firm forward a complaint to another firm, and when do the time limits start for the firm that receives it?

**Gold:** DISP 1.7.1, DISP 1.7.3 (all needed)

**Acceptable answer:** With reasonable grounds that the other firm is solely or jointly responsible, forwarding promptly in writing and telling the complainant why with contact details. The receiving firm's standard time limits run from the date it receives the forwarded complaint.

> **DISP 1.7.1R:** Forwarding a complaint A respondent that has reasonable grounds to be satisfied that another respondent may be solely or jointly responsible for the matter alleged in a complaint may forward the complaint, or the relevant part of it, in writing to that other respondent, provided it: (1) does so promptly; (2) informs the complainant promptly in a final response of why the complaint has been forwarded by it to the other respondent, and of the other respondent's contact details; and (3) where jointly responsible for the fault alleged in the complaint, it complies with its own obligations under this chapter in respect of that part of the complaint it has not forwarded.

> **DISP 1.7.3G:** On receiving a forwarded complaint, the standard time limits will apply from the date on which the respondent receives the forwarded complaint.

## a38: needs every listed provision

**Q:** What must a product manufacturer do before launching a product, and while it is on the market?

**Gold:** PRIN 2A.3.2, PRIN 2A.3.7 (all needed)

**Acceptable answer:** Run an approval process for the product (and significant adaptations) before it is marketed or distributed, then review it regularly for events affecting risk to the target market, including whether it still meets the target market's needs and whether distribution remains appropriate.

> **PRIN 2A.3.2R:** Manufacturer product governance arrangements A manufacturer must maintain, operate and review a process for the approval of: (1) a product; and (2) significant adaptations of a product, in each case before it is marketed or distributed to retail customers.

> **PRIN 2A.3.7R:** Manufacturer: review A manufacturer must regularly review its products taking into account any event that could materially affect the potential risk to the target market. In doing so, the manufacturer must assess at least the following: (1) whether the product meets the identified needs, characteristics and objectives of the target market, including identified needs, characteristics and objectives of retail customers in the target market with characteristics of vulnerability; and (2) whether the intended distribution strategy remains appropriate, including whether the product is being distributed to the target market or reaching retail customers outside the target market.
